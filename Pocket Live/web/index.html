Pocket / Live

Connect to Ableton Live

Pocket / Live needs a connection to your Live Set before you can control it from this browser.

  1. Download and unzip Pocket Live. Drag Pocket Live.amxd onto an audio track in Ableton Live.
  2. In the device, scan its QR code or click Open Controller.
