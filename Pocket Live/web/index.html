Pocket / Live

Connect to Ableton Live

Pocket / Live needs a connection to your Live Set before you can control it from this browser.

  1. On the computer running Ableton Live, download and unzip Pocket Live. Drag Pocket Live.amxd onto an audio track.
  2. Scan the device’s QR code with your iPad or phone. To connect on the same computer, click Open Controller in the device.
